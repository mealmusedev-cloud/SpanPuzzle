iPhone and iPad

A side-by-side layout on iPad, with keyboard shortcuts and trackpad support.

How to play

Every board is a tangle of strings woven over and under each other. Tap a string with nothing lying on top of it and it slides away. Tap one that's still pinned under another, and you lose a heart. Clear every string to finish the board.
